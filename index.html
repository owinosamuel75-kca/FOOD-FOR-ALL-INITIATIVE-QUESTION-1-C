Our Mission

Food for All Initiative is a non-governmental organization based in Nairobi, Kenya. Our mission is to fight food insecurity by providing food assistance to vulnerable families and communities. We believe everyone deserves access to nutritious food.

Our Programmes

  • Food donation and distribution.
  • Community feeding programmes.
  • Nutrition awareness and education.
