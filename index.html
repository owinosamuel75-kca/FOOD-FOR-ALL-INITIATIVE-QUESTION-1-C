Our Mission

Food for All Initiative is a non-governmental organization based in Nairobi, Kenya. Our mission is to fight food insecurity by providing food assistance to vulnerable families and communities.

Our Programmes

  • Food donation and distribution.
  • Community feeding programmes.
  • Nutrition awareness and education.
